Demo Data Notice

Prototype only. Punchin by Wulden is a static browser demo, not an employer-operated attendance system. The sample employees and seeded events are fictional.

What the demo stores in your browser:

  • Profile: the name you enter or select.
  • Selfie: a compressed image if you permit camera access and capture a photo.
  • Location: device coordinates and approximate accuracy if you permit location access; otherwise a visibly labeled simulated location.
  • Events: clock-in/out time, optional note, and geofence comparison.

Storage and access: data remains in localStorage on this browser; it is not encrypted or uploaded by the demo. Anyone with access to this browser profile may inspect it. The visible demo PIN does not protect data. Clear it with “Reset demo” or by clearing site data.

Do not use real employee data. There is no production retention policy, identity verification, server audit trail, or legal compliance certification. A real deployment needs an employer-approved privacy notice, security controls, and a retention policy reviewed for its jurisdiction.
